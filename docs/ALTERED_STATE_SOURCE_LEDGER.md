# Altered State Reality Engines — Source Ledger

## Purpose

This ledger documents what informed `src/data/realityAlteredStates.ts`.

The cards are **creative phenomenology engines for songwriting**. They are not medical guidance, drug education instructions, or claims that every person experiences a substance in the same way.

No dosage, acquisition, preparation, administration, redosing, combination, or optimization material was imported into runtime cards.

## Source tiers

### Tier A — Ghost Erowid creative archive

Primary creative source.

This material is valuable because it already converts recurring experience-report motifs into operational systems, locations, transitions, entities, and rendering metaphors. It is treated as **project canon / creative synthesis**, not as scientific proof.

Important caution: portions of the Ghost archive contain speculative cosmology, synthetic taxonomies, exact-looking source counts, and interpretive claims generated during prior research sessions. Those details are useful for art direction but are **not automatically factual**.

### Tier B — external scientific / regulatory material

Used to sanity-check broad phenomenological categories such as:

- derealization / altered self-awareness
- dissociation / body detachment
- time distortion
- synesthesia
- ego dissolution
- entity-encounter reports
- social-affiliative effects
- delirium / confusion / hallucinations
- complex sleep behavior / amnesia

External sources are not used to validate the Ghost cosmology itself.

### Tier C — user-canon / deliberately fictional abstraction

Used where the creative mechanic is the point and the source literature only supports a broader family of effects.

Examples:

- **SALVIA OBJECT ETERNITY** — the Ghost archive supports page/wheel merging, reality folding, identity disturbance, and many-lives/page motifs; the exact “I have always been a highway rock for fourteen billion lifetimes” mechanic is a deliberately sharpened creative abstraction.
- **AMBIEN-AWAKE DOMESTIC DREAM LOGIC** — FDA material supports complex behavior while not fully awake plus later amnesia; “the lamp needed legal counsel” is intentionally fictionalized domestic dream logic.
- **NITROUS COSMIC PUNCHLINE** — the Ghost vault strongly foregrounds revelation-loss and cosmic-joke motifs; controlled literature supports euphoria, dreamy detachment, body-image change, and time alteration, not a universal cosmic-joke phenomenon.

# External scientific / regulatory sources

## Salvia divinorum

### Johnson et al. / qualitative experimental phenomenology

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25691501/

Useful broad support:

- rapid and intense altered state
- marked changes in auditory, visual, and interoceptive input
- loss of normal awareness of self and surroundings
- delusion-like phenomena

### González et al. / recreational-user subjective effects

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16720081/

Useful broad support:

- highly modified perception of external reality and self
- intense derealization
- impaired interaction with self/surroundings

Important: these sources do **not** establish the Ghost archive's Wheel, Book, Zipper, or object-eternity taxonomy as universal scientific facts.

## DMT

### Davis et al. 2020 — entity encounter survey

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32345112/

Useful support:

- survey respondents reported apparently autonomous entity encounters
- many attributed consciousness / intelligence to encountered entities
- message reception was commonly reported in that selected survey population

Important: the study is a retrospective survey of people reporting entity encounters. It does not establish that entities objectively exist.

# Runtime safety / fidelity rules

1. Do not generate dose, route, sourcing, preparation, mixing, redosing, or optimization instructions.
2. Do not assume an experience is universal because it appears in the Ghost archive.
3. Treat project cosmology as creative canon unless separately verified.
4. Prefer mechanisms over drug-name stereotypes.
5. Do not turn dangerous delirium or complex sleep behavior into a recommendation.
6. Allow bizarre combinations in fiction, but do not imply that combining real substances will reproduce them.
7. The strongest cards should still work if the drug name is hidden; the mechanism should carry the creativity.
