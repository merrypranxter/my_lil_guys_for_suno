# GENERATIVE PHYSICS IDEA VAULT

This is the long-term pile for viable Lil Guys / Suno mechanisms discovered during idea-mining sessions.

## Core principle

Do not treat every preset as a genre recipe.

A **recipe** says: start with these settings.

A **generative physics** says: these are the laws this musical universe obeys; the song is the exhaust produced when those laws run.

Keep three implementation classes distinct:

- **PROMPT-NATIVE** — can be expressed directly as Suno-facing instructions and existing Lil Guys / Composition Lab state.
- **STATEFUL** — Lil Guys itself must maintain a ledger, history, inheritance chain, threshold, or resource state across sections/runs.
- **AUDIO-NATIVE** — concept is valuable, but literal execution belongs in future stem/DSP/audio-processing infrastructure. Until then, prompt only the audible consequence, never pretend Suno is literally running the DSP/math.

# CURRENT BUILD QUEUE — WIDE FIRST BATCH

The immediate target is a deliberately varied set, not twenty versions of the same “experimental” trick.

## Job 1 — MOUTH OPERA
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Human body only. No conventional instruments. Lungs/breath, consonants, vowels, tongue, teeth, lips, whispers, heartbeat, claps, and operatic voices receive separate musical jurisdictions. Speech can become song; song can become percussion; percussion can become choir. One sustained operatic vowel/interval remains an anchor.

## Job 2 — DETROIT ASSEMBLY-LINE POSSESSION
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Immaculate Motown-like bass pocket, dry snare/claps, guitar punctuation, piano/horns/group vocals — but each system obeys an incompatible operational rule. Bass remains civilization while harmony/voices mutate around it.

## Job 3 — BLUEGRASS PARTICLE ACCELERATOR
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Banjo/fiddle/mandolin/dobro/upright bass with separate temporal laws. Moderate base pulse, absurd activity rate, clean acoustic articulation, hocket voices, and occasional near-total exposure windows.

## Job 4 — LOOSE-MOUTH POCKET MONSTER
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
The band is an immovable funk pocket. The lead voice is allowed to be rhythmically socially unacceptable: late, early, slurred, muttered, cracked, half-sung, register-jumping, yet repeatedly landing at structurally exact points.

## Job 5 — RUBBER-THROAT FUNK CABARET
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Vocal anatomy behaves like a lead synth: baritone/falsetto/mutter/nasal squeal/register breaks alter available melodic behavior while clipped guitar, elastic bass, dry drums, and theatrical punctuation remain disciplined.

## Job 6 — OPERA IN A TOOL SHED
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Technically committed operatic voice over tiny crude physical accompaniment (tack piano, washboard, jaw harp, fiddle scratch, accordion wheeze, handclap). Midway, structural responsibility inverts: cheap instruments acquire operatic scale while voice collapses to breath/barks/syllables.

## Job 7 — THE CHOIR HAS UNIONIZED
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Backing voices refuse decorative status. Choir sections acquire percussion, bass-entry, timing, or formal authority; the lead gradually becomes accompaniment to the rebellion.

## Job 8 — MUTATING CANON
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
A round where Voice 2 transforms Voice 1, Voice 3 receives Voice 2 rather than the original, etc. The canon has heredity. Descendants remain related but are not identical copies.

## Job 9 — SIR MOTIF EPIDEMIC
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
Susceptible → Infected → Recovered musical populations. A motif infects adjacent performers, mutates as it spreads, and burns itself out because recovered performers become immune.

## Job 10 — LRU NOTE CACHE
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
The piece has a strict finite pitch/motif cache. Introducing a new item evicts the least recently used item; evicted material becomes unavailable and harmony must rebuild around the vacancy.

## Job 11 — M/M/1 MUSICAL BOTTLENECK
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Polyphonic events must pass through a single-note processing gate. Chords become rapid queues/arpeggios; event backlog smears time; excess events may be dropped or deferred.

## Job 12 — DOUBLE-ENTRY ENERGY LEDGER
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
Density, loudness, attention, harmonic tension, or register occupancy are finite budgets. Adding energy to one jurisdiction forces subtraction elsewhere.

## Job 13 — ITERATED PRISONER'S DUET
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
Two melodic agents repeatedly cooperate or defect based on each other’s previous behavior. Their relationship history changes harmony/dissonance over time.

## Job 14 — TELEOLOGICAL BOOTSTRAP SONG
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Lock the ending first. Earlier events must become increasingly necessary ancestors of that terminal state. The beginning sounds oddly predictive because later structure selects its causes.

## Job 15 — VALID-BUT-WRONG MAXIMIZER
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE  
Macro structure remains strongly valid and graspable while micro choices maximize strangeness within legality. Candidate global control: **WRONGNESS INSIDE VALIDITY**.

## Job 16 — SCAR-TISSUE CHORUS
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
Every recall of a chorus/motif inherits one permanent scar from what happened since the last recall. No pristine copy-paste returns.

## Job 17 — SILENCE ECONOMY
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
Not playing earns currency. Dense/rare/premium events cost currency. Overactive parts go broke; long-silent parts gain authority.

## Job 18 — MUSICAL IMMUNE SYSTEM
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
The anchor defines “self.” Excessive familiarity triggers mutation/defense. High autoimmunity makes the song eventually attack its own hook.

## Job 19 — RUMOR NETWORK
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
Only Performer A knows the original. A teaches B; B teaches C; C teaches D. Each transmission mutates. The final descendant returns and overwrites the original.

## Job 20 — PHASE-CHANGE SONG
**Class:** PROMPT-NATIVE / STATEFUL LATER  
Parameters accumulate continuously until a critical threshold causes abrupt global reorganization. Knobs need not be smooth: 72→73 can cross a phase boundary and change the entire topology.

# LYRIC / LANGUAGE ENGINES TO BUILD

These are independent of musical style and should eventually be selectable or breedable.

### FALSE LIMERICK
Steal limerick locomotion — bounce, stress expectation, asymmetric phrase feel, short-short resolution — without requiring rhyme, five lines, or a joke.

### TRUE / MUTATING ROUND
Write material that can survive staggered canonical entrances. Optional heredity mode mutates each generation instead of returning to the original source.

### MULTI-CLOCK ROUND
Same text/phrase, but each entering voice experiences duration differently: stretched vowels, compressed consonants, fixed syllable lengths, redistributed timing.

### DEADWOOD MOUTH
Profanity is not random decoration. Curse families have grammatical jobs: pickup filler, clause interruption, intensifier, meter lengthener, failed-thought marker, call-and-response punctuation.

### PROFANITY AS PUNCTUATION PHYSICS
A more formal Deadwood mode where swear types map to explicit prosodic/rhythmic functions.

### SEMANTIC DEBT
Lines may introduce unexplained information and accumulate debt. Later lines must pay earlier debts. If debt becomes excessive, the lyric collapses into brutally literal explanation and resets.

### WORD EXTINCTION
Important words have limited lifetime counts. Once extinct, the lyric cannot synonym-cheat; it must communicate through consequences, syntax, phonetics, omission, or changed perspective.

### PARASITIC LYRIC FORM
Lyric forms infect one another operationally. Example: limerick stress infects prose without rhyme; a round later infects the cadence; profanity infects weak syllable positions only.

### PHONETIC ENGINE
Hard consonants = percussion; nasals = drone; open vowels = sustain; rolled consonants = acceleration; dense syllables = compressed runs; long vowels = stretched time; heavy syllables = bass weight.

# ADDITIONAL VIABLE PROMPT-NATIVE PHYSICS

### CMYK / HALFTONE INTERFERENCE
Four simple structural “plates” are slightly misregistered so the composite produces a phantom fifth rhythm. Literal phase/sample-rate DSP belongs in AUDIO-NATIVE; prompt-native version uses offset accent maps and near-aligned cycles.

### TECTONIC SUBDUCTION
Two rhythmic plates of different cycle length accumulate compression debt until a release/reset event.

### PROGRAMMED APOPTOSIS
Parts have lifespans and dismantle themselves in a prescribed order rather than simply fading out.

### GIT MERGE CONFLICT
Two valid versions of the same section coexist. Agreement passes cleanly; disagreement forces overlap/conflict until a resolution rule selects or recombines them.

### JUST-IN-TIME EFFECT SUPPLY
Spatial/decorative functions “arrive late.” Prompt-native version stages delayed consequence: dry attack first, tail/sub/harmony shipment later.

### GARBAGE COLLECTION
At checkpoints, inactive/non-load-bearing material is culled and cannot quietly return.

### PROOF-OF-WORK GATES
Major transitions require an audible holding state before permission to continue. Literal hashes are not required; the law is “transition waits on gate resolution.”

### NAVIER-STOKES / TURBULENCE THRESHOLD
Density remains smooth below threshold; crossing it causes a sudden regime change into turbulent articulation/noise; return requires dropping below threshold.

### BATESON DOUBLE BIND
Two absolute musical constraints cannot both be satisfied. The evasion strategy becomes the composition.

### CAUSAL DEPENDENCY GRAVITY
Events gain “mass” according to how many future events depend on them. High-dependency anchors warp surrounding pitch/timing/space/attention.

### EXCEPTIONAL-POINT MORPHOGENESIS
Small parameter changes do nothing until a critical point is crossed, then topology/state changes abruptly.

### LOSSY ALIASING
Destroy representational detail until two unrelated musical entities collide; reconstruct without restoring the lost distinction, allowing hybrid behavior to survive.

### MISSING FUNCTION
Delete one foundational musical service — timekeeper, tonic establishment, repetition, melody carrier, section marker — and do not appoint a replacement. The rest of the ensemble adapts locally around the vacancy.

### FERMENTATION ENGINE
Repetition is food. Repeating one kind of material metabolizes it into another jurisdiction: repeated rhythm may generate harmony; repeated lyric may break into phonemes.

### RIGHT-OF-WAY MUSIC
Musical events obey priority/yield/collision law rather than simple layering. Collision produces an explicit recovery protocol.

### DARWIN'S SHITTY LITTLE VERSE
Seed tiny musical organisms, apply finite resources and fitness criteria, allow reproduction/hybridization/extinction, and keep the lineage whose descendants open the richest territory.

### THE BOTTLENECK
Periodically force the available musical population through extreme survivorship; everything afterward must descend from what survived.

### MUSICAL GERRYMANDERING
Redistribute musical territory/authority so minority agents gain disproportionate control; periodically redraw the districts.

### THE SONG LEARNS YOUR HANDS
Manual user edits crystallize touched parameters while untouched parameters become freer to mutate. Repeated correction becomes a signal of sacredness.

### ONE BEAUTIFUL THING / EVERYBODY ELSE MISBEHAVES
Protect one gorgeous invariant — bass line, fiddle tune, vocal phrase — while all other jurisdictions are allowed aggressive mutation.

### VOCAL ZOO
Instrumental system remains legible while each singer belongs to a radically different vocal physiology/technique system.

### NO DRUMMER, STILL FUCKING DANCE
Dedicated drums/percussion are forbidden. Pulse migrates into bass attacks, guitar chops, consonants, claps, breaths, key noise, bow attacks, etc.

### BUBBLEGUM BASS CULT
Bright, ecstatic, hooky party-funk baseline where repetition recruits additional rhythmic/vocal participants until the groove becomes a tiny social system.

### BLUEGRASS TRANSLATION MACHINE
Convert an existing arrangement into acoustic string-band functions without literal instrument replacement. Preserve causal jobs, not surface timbre.

### VAUDEVILLE FAILURE CASCADE
Cue-driven theatrical arrangement where swallowed syllables, held vowels, laughter, or interruptions causally change orchestration/form rather than merely decorate it.

### TWO HEART RATES
Separate base BPM from activity pulse. Moderate skeleton; frantic bloodstream.

# AUDIO-NATIVE PARKING LOT

These remain valuable. Do not fake literal execution in prompt-only mode.

### Literal auditory moiré / phase-rotated stem plates
Requires deterministic stem phase/sample-rate/DSP control.

### Literal packet-loss / retransmission engine
Requires buffer/state playback control for true dropped/reinserted audio packets.

### Reaction-diffusion spectrogram carving
Requires synthesis/DSP or offline spectral processing for literal activator/inhibitor pattern formation.

### GLSL-to-audio mapping
Requires generated waveforms or DSP graph from shader/coordinate functions.

### Literal latency-varied effect supply chain
Requires separate effect buses/stem timing.

### Literal cryptographic proof-of-work transition latency
Requires runtime compute gate connected to playback.

### Observation-crystallized interactive audio
Requires regeneration/state tied to playhead/listener behavior.

### Iterated model-collapse lineage exhibition
Requires repeated audio-to-audio generation chain and saved lineage.

### Literal lossy stem collision/reconstruction
Requires audio encoding/destruction/resynthesis.

# SYSTEM / UI IDEAS

### Recipe vs Generative Physics
Keep “Recipe” for configuration presets. Add “Generative Physics” as a broader category for laws that govern evolution/state.

### Implementation badge
Every physics/preset may carry:
- PROMPT-NATIVE
- STATEFUL
- AUDIO-NATIVE

### Phase-transition controls
Allow some controls to use threshold behavior rather than linear interpolation.

### WRONGNESS INSIDE VALIDITY
Global slider: preserve macro legibility while maximizing legal micro-strangeness.

### AUTOIMMUNITY
Global or recipe-specific control for how aggressively repeated familiar material triggers mutation.

### CACHE SIZE
Finite vocabulary/motif memory pressure.

### TRANSMISSION R0
Motif infection rate.

### FORGIVENESS
Relationship-memory decay in game-theoretic duets.

### SILENCE VALUE
How quickly absence earns future authority.

### GENERATIONAL DRIFT
How much each canonical/telephone-chain descendant may mutate.

# RULE FOR FUTURE BOREDOM

When Merry asks for new shit:

1. Pull from this vault before inventing from scratch.
2. Prefer mechanisms not yet implemented.
3. Prefer a wide spread across vocal, groove, memory, resource, form, temporal, social, and failure systems.
4. Do not let successful ideas domesticate the app.
5. A new idea must either:
   - introduce a genuinely new operating law,
   - create a new interaction between existing laws,
   - or make a previously theoretical AUDIO-NATIVE idea executable.
6. If it is only “genre A + genre B,” throw it back in the fucking pond.
